# About The Project

This contract allows users to sign up for the raffle to win an NFT. A user or a group can enter multiple times. Duplicate addresses are forbidden. Users are allowed to get a refund. sometimes, the raffle will be able to draw a winner and be minted a random NFT. The owner of the protocol will set a fee to take a cut of the `value`, and the rest of the funds will be sent to the winner of the NFT.
